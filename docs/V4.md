# Road to 4.0 (read with ARCHITECTURE.md)

Design decisions below are made by the owner. Do not change them; flag concerns in your report.

## Combat rules
- **Headshots.** Top-down, so the head is a precision zone: a ray passing within `HEAD_RADIUS` 0.2 m of the target centre. Damage times `weapon.headMult` (default 1.4; sniper 1; shotgun about 1.1). `hit`/`kill` events carry `head:true`. Aim assist stops at the body edge and never pulls toward the centre.
- **Slide dodge.** Hit radius is 0.72x while sliding.
- **Auto-reload.** No button. Reload at empty; also top off after 1.2 s without firing or aiming when the mag is under 70%. Firing with rounds left cancels the reload.
- **LMG heat** (replaces the steadying `settle` behaviour). Heat bar full at 40 straight rounds. Cools after 0.4 s idle, full to empty in about 2.5 s. At full: locked about 2.2 s; pistol swap allowed. Accuracy loosens only in the last quarter of the bar. Reload drops to about 4.5 s.
- **Turn-speed cap.** Heavy guns have a max rad/s of aim rotation while aiming or firing (`weapon.turnCap`, LMG most severe). A flanker should beat a planted LMG.
- **Firing reveals you on the enemy minimap** for about 1.5 s unless suppressed (Wave 2 attachment). `PlayerView.firingT` > 0.
- **Equipment.** One tactical (button replaces reload) and one lethal (new button). Counts refill on respawn. See `js/shared/catalog.js`.

## Scorestreak rules (final)
Score: kill 100, headshot +25, assist 50. Score persists through death. Each player has 3 slots sorted by cost; streaks do not stack.
1. As score rises, a slot that is not held and not blocked is granted when score reaches its cost.
2. If no slot is left that can still be earned above the current score, score is **capped** and stops rising.
3. Using a streak while capped: score resets to 0 and all blocks clear.
4. Using a streak while not capped: no reset. If score is already at or past that streak's cost, that slot is **blocked** until the next reset.

Example: hold all three, capped, use Turret -> score 0. At 400 use UAV (cost 300) -> no reset, UAV blocked. At 500 Turret granted -> capped. Use it -> 0, UAV earnable again.

## Added data shapes
**Loadout** `normalizeLoadout()` in catalog. Roster entries gain `loadout`, with `primary`/`camo` kept in sync.

**Input** becomes `{mx,mz,aim,aiming,fire,sprint,slide,swap,tactical,lethal,streak}`. `tactical`,`lethal` are edge booleans; `streak` is -1 or slot 0..2 for one read. No `reload`.

**PlayerView** gains `score, capped, slots:[{kind,cost,held,blocked}], heat (0..1), overheated, head (headshot count), assists, firingT, tacticalLeft, lethalLeft, tactical, lethal`.

**GameView** gains `projectiles:[{id,kind,x,z,y}]`, `smokes:[{id,x,z,r}]`, `turrets:[{id,team,owner,x,z,aim,hp}]`, `gunships:[{team,owner,x,z,aim}]`, `mode`.

**Commands** humans send to the host: `{cmd:'streak',slot}`, `{cmd:'throw',slot:'tactical'|'lethal',ox,oz,aim}`, `{cmd:'loadout',loadout}`. Host calls `sim.applyCommand(id,msg)`; guests send through `net.sendShot(msg)`. Sim validates counts.

**New events** `{t:'throw',id,kind,ox,oz,aim}`, `{t:'bang',kind,x,z,r}` (grenade/flash burst), `{t:'flashed',id,amount}`, `{t:'heal',id}`, `{t:'overheat',id}`, `{t:'turret',id,team,x,z}`, `{t:'turret_down',id,x,z}`, `{t:'gunship',team,owner}`, `{t:'gunship_shot',ox,oz,x,z}`, `{t:'turret_shot',ox,oz,x,z}`.

Smoke blocks vision: use `hasLineOfSight(map,ax,az,bx,bz,smokes)` and `smokeBlocks` from geometry.js.

## Progression (Wave 2, one save with `version`, migrate old data)
Player level 1 to 50, then Prestige to rank 10: level resets, everything is kept, earns a badge. Weapon level 1 to 30 unlocks attachments. Weapon prestige is cosmetic only: one shared set of prestige cosmetics, each unlocked per weapon by prestiging that weapon. Camo challenges (kills, headshots, longshots, slide kills), mastery needs all. Loadouts, not classes. Export/import save code.

## Waves
1 Combat. 2 Progression. 3 Content: three more maps with own lighting and weather, map vote, six more guns, Kill Confirmed and Domination, unlockable operators. 4 Polish and juice: real killcam replays, animations, death effects, gun audio overhaul, firing range, rejoin, host migration, iPhone performance. Battle royale is out of scope.

# Wave 2 contract: progression and loadouts

**Attachments** (`js/shared/attachments.js`). Slots: `optic, barrel, mag, grip`. Max one per slot, all four usable. Data-only table of `{id,slot,name,desc,unlock(weapon level),mods}`, each with a real tradeoff, suppressor included (barrel: hides `firingT` reveal, costs range). LMG heat-sink barrel: more rounds before overheat, slower aim. `applyAttachments(weaponDef, attachments)` returns an effective def, memoised. Attachments are the only stat bonuses in the game.

**Progress v2** (`js/ui/progress.js`, one versioned localStorage save; migrates v1 keeping name, XP, kills, camos). Player level 1 to 50, Prestige to rank 10 resets level and keeps every unlock and earns a badge. Per weapon: XP, level 1 to 30, weapon prestige 0 to 3 (resets that weapon's level, keeps its attachments, cosmetic rewards only). Shared prestige cosmetic set in data, each item requires a given prestige on that specific weapon: camos, tracer colors, kill effects. Camo challenges per weapon: kills, headshots, longshots, slide kills, equipment-free; mastery camo requires completing them all. All base weapons stay available from level 1 so new friends can play anything; player level unlocks perks/equipment/streaks gently, the top of the list by about level 15.
**5 named loadouts**, editable, selectable between lives. Save export/import as a compact copy-paste code.

**Awards** (`js/game/awards.js`): `class MatchAwards { constructor({localId,team,loadout}); onEvents(events,view); finish(view) -> results }`. Tracks kills, headshots, longshots, slide kills, equipment kills, weapon XP, medals. `finish` applies them to Progress and returns `{xp breakdown, level before/after, weapon level changes, unlocks[], ...}` merged into the Match results passed to `onEnd`. Lead calls it from Match.

**Roster** entries gain `badge:{prestige,level}`, shown in lobby, scoreboard and kill feed. Loadouts travel in roster `loadout`, including `attachments`, `tracer`, `killFx`.

# Wave 3 contract: content

**Guns.** `WEAPON_IDS` in constants is now 12: new primaries `burst` (three-round burst rifle), `pdw` (compact very high RPM, hard recoil), `autoshot` (drum auto shotgun, short range), `dmr` (semi-auto marksman, two-shot mid range); new secondaries `revolver` (slow, huge damage, headshot king) and `launcher` (`explosive:{radius,damage}` shot resolved by the host as an explosion at the ray end, 1 round, huge reload). `loadout.secondary` picks any secondary. Invented names. Each one distinct in handling, model, sound and recoil; same chunky style; camos and attachments apply. Progression and menus iterate `WEAPON_IDS`; new guns unlock through player level.

**Maps.** One file per map in `js/shared/maps/<id>.js`, each `export default build()` giving the same shape as Railyard plus `theme` (a render key) and `points: { dom:[{id,x,z,r}x3], hard:[{id,x,z,r}...] }`. `js/shared/map.js` registers them in `MAPS` (Railyard included, moved to its file). Three new maps with different layouts and sightlines, all symmetric: `harbor` (storm-lashed night docks, rain, lightning), `neon` (tight night market alleys, neon signage), `dust` (bright desert afternoon, sand haze). Longest clean sightline around 30 m where possible, no spawn-trap. Bots use the existing nav builder.

**Modes** are data in `js/shared/modes.js`; Sim constructor takes `mode`. View fields: `tags:[{id,x,z,team}]` (kc), `points:[{id,x,z,r,owner:-1|0|1,capture:-1..1,contested}]` (dom/hard), `scoreLimit`. Events: `{t:'tag',kind:'confirm'|'deny',id,x,z}`, `{t:'capture',point,team}`, `{t:'hard_move',id}`. Score awards from catalog SCORE; bots must play the objective.

**Map vote.** After the lobby is ready the host starts a vote of 3 random map and mode pairs (10 s); solo gets a pick directly. Rematch reruns it.

**Operators.** Character variants (silhouette and color differences on the low-poly rig, chosen in the menu, seen in matches); several unlocked by level or challenge. `loadout.operator` id.

# Wave 4 contract: polish, juice, reliability

**Controlled throws.** Holding Tactical or Lethal enters aim mode: drag from the button sets direction and distance (the same thumb), a trajectory arc with bounces and landing marker shows in the world, release throws, dragging back onto the button cancels. A quick tap throws along current aim at default range. `controls.read()` gains `throwAim: null | {slot:'tactical'|'lethal', aim, dist}` while held; on release the edge fires with `throwDist`, `throwAim` set. Command becomes `{cmd:'throw',slot,ox,oz,aim,dist}`. Base max 13 m; new perk **Strong Arm** (`strongarm`) reaches 19 m. `previewThrow(map,ox,oz,aim,dist,kind)` in equipment.js returns polyline points and landing, matching the real throw exactly. Match puts the preview on `view.throwPreview`; renderer draws it.

**Bots must earn information.** Sound reveals a rough area only, with error growing with distance. Sim asserts no bot reacts to a silent, out-of-sight player absent UAV or a delayed teammate callout, proven by a test.

**Firing range.** Solo mode `range` on its own small map: stationary and strafing dummies at marked distances, instant reloads and a live DPS/TTK readout. Reached from the menu; loadouts and attachments apply.

**Reliability.** Rejoin: a dropped guest can re-enter the same code and retake the slot from its bot, keeping stats. Host migration: if the host disappears, the next peer takes over using its latest snapshot (`Sim.fromSnapshot`), bots rebuilt, the match continues with a brief notice. `match.promoteToHost()` (Lead).

**Killcam.** Real replay of the final kill from buffered views at match end (a slowed view from the killer's side), and a short replay after each death for the player (skippable, never delays respawn).
