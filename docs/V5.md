# Wave 5: system overhaul (owner decisions; do not change them)

Read docs/ARCHITECTURE.md and docs/V4.md first. Owner wants this content-heavy, and bugs found along the way fixed.

## Combat
- **Headshots are an instant kill on every gun** (explosives excepted). Head zone stays small (`HEAD_RADIUS`, tune down if needed), aim assist never pulls toward it.
- **Semi-autos and snipers fire while held**, at their own cadence. No gun requires releasing the trigger. Launcher included.
- **Every gun has a tighter falloff curve and a hard max range**; numbers visible in the Gunsmith.
- **Bullets and cover.** Buildings, containers, walls, borders stop everything. Waist-high cover (crate, barrier) stops light rounds (SMG, pistol, shotgun class); rifles, LMGs and snipers penetrate it at reduced damage (`ray.pen` damage multiplier; `weapon.penetrate`). Audit for any real wall-clip: shot origin versus walls when hugging or peeking, rewind positions, tracer endpoints.
- **Throws are fluid**: no pause or hitch. Find the cause (first-use buffer or shader compile counts); keep moving and shooting.

## Scorestreaks
Score **persists through death** (Wave 6 owner fix, restoring the V4 rule); held streaks stay. Costs are high enough that streaks take real play to earn: measured on bot matches, a good player earns about one mid-list streak (Sentry to Overwatch) per match and the top of the list rarely. Airspace limit: one Gunship per team, a cap on total air streaks at once, per-streak limits where sensible. A blocked streak is kept and the tile shows why.

## Content targets
Guns about 24 across categories (`weapon.category`): Assault, SMG, Shotgun, LMG, Marksman, Sniper, Pistol, Launcher, each with real identity. Attachments: deeper per-category options with real tradeoffs. Perks about 15 in three tiers (one pick per tier: `perks:[t1,t2,t3]`, COD-style). Streaks about 10. Maps: three more, plus rendered snapshot art for every map.
