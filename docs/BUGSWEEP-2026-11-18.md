### 1a. Flaky tests fixed
1. **sim-unlocks quick: "operator courier: 'ex:intel' never banked".** Quick cut every non-round match at 420 s; a bot
   extraction match ends around 460 to 480 s and squads bank their contracts on the late helicopters, so a cut match
   banked almost nothing. Quick now plays extraction to its end like the round modes (one seed): Secure Intel is
   banked 8 times per quick run.
2. **ui-clans-test timed out tapping Leave.** Not the overlay: after results the host's rematch countdown opens the
   next vote, and a vote takes over a guest's screen wherever they are, the Clan screen included, so the tap raced it.
   That is the room working as designed. The guest now leaves the room from wherever it is (results, vote or lobby)
   before the menus, and the host tab is closed (it played on alone and starved the guest's taps). Also: the vote card
   is picked by its mode name (a description could name another mode, and a Showdown or Ground War card left the two
   humans with no bot beside them, so the chatter checks had no teammate), and the "chatter off" check counts its own
   line (other rows fade out meanwhile).
3. **sim-gunfight** expected at most four Showdown maps; Millbrook is a fifth (docs/MODES.md updated).
4. **sim-seasons** counted 12 feat emblems; Dead Frequency (1118-6) is the thirteenth.
5. **ui-events-test** expected the weekly events entry on the What's new sheet, which shows the newest three: later
   builds pushed it off. It now checks the newest entry is on the sheet and weekly events is in the unseen list.
6. **ui-history-test** Showdown: its "round is live" wait was `|| true`, so on a slow machine the kills landed in the
   freeze (no damage) and no round was recorded. It waits for the live round, then for the round to end.
7. **Vote taps under load** (ui-flow-test, ui-br-net-test, ui-boards-net-test, ui-sweep-net): the vote card's enter
   animation kept it "unstable" for Playwright's whole tap timeout, past the vote's own clock, so nobody voted and a
   random card won (sweep-net's TDM case played Rebound). The card is now clicked through the DOM once present (the
   same handler a tap reaches); flow-test's vote and match-start waits are 40 to 60 s.
8. ui-br-test's results wait is 90 s (the end banner and final kill hold come first) and prints the match state if it
   still times out.

## Fixed (game)
1. **BR drop map: with a controller the Jump button slid off the screen** (at 667x375 it sat at x 706 to 906). The
   controller's focus ring sets `position: relative`, and the map's Jump button still carried the HUD lane's
   `left`/`bottom` offsets, so focusing it moved it by them. js/ui/br-hud.js.
2. **Objective markers stuck in the top-left corner on big Ground War maps** (Headquarters on Expedition): a point
   behind the camera's plane (about 1.4 camera heights below the screen) was skipped, so a marker never placed sat at
   0,0, and one placed before stayed where it was. It is now projected with |w| and pushed off-screen, so it rides the
   edge and points at the objective. js/ui/hud.js.
3. **Boards > Clans: a long clan name ran off the screen** in the detail panel at every width (16 wide letters in the
   stencil face). It truncates with an ellipsis. css/clans.css.
