## Fixed
1. **Controller: the Buy/Open, Take and Jump prompts left the screen.** Controller mode set `position: relative` on
   them to anchor the A glyph, but they are placed absolutely in the prompt lane, so they fell to the top edge (the
   Graveyard Shift Buy button sat above the screen on Neon at 667x375; BR's Take and Jump the same). js/ui/gamepad.js.
2. **Graveyard Shift: a host migration reset everyone's points, perks, plates and gun tiers.** promoteToHost builds a
   new Sim whose players start blank, and ZmRules.restore kept what it found. It now carries each player's state over
   from the guest's mirror. The sim test reused the guest's own players, which hid it; it now also migrates onto a
   fresh Sim. js/sim/zombies.js, dev/sim-zombies.mjs.
3. **Objective markers sat on strip plates that arrive mid-match** (Headquarters' HQ marker on top of "Defend your HQ"):
   the strip rows were measured once, empty, at the start. A ResizeObserver keeps their rects. js/ui/hud.js.
4. **Showdown: an objective marker stuck in the top-left corner** before overtime (the overtime flag's marker, with no
   point to place it), and it would have stayed where it was after overtime. Markers with no point are hidden.
5. **Empty "0" streak tiles and a score counter in modes whose kit has no streaks** (Showdown, One in the Chamber, the
   infected). They hide when the dealt kit has none and come back if it has some.
6. **Graveyard Shift results** (owner notes): "Round 18" in the header and hero instead of "18 - 18"; the record panel's
   Best escape and Gravewarden values no longer wrap out of the panel ("R18", "Down", the card in the label); no final
   kill replay or Final kill card after an escape (the card says "Out on the exfil on round N"). A `results-zm` scene
   in dev/ui-gallery.html, checked by the overflow test at every size.
7. **Graveyard Shift difficulty and pay** (owner notes: solo with bots escaped round 18 untouched, +28k XP, a gun maxed in
   one run): harder past round 10 and per person in the crew, about a third of the XP, a tenth of the weapon XP with a
   per-gun cap. Numbers, method and before/after in docs/ZOMBIES.md "Difficulty and pay"; `dev/sim-zm-runs.mjs`.
