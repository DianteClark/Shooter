# V7: veterans, Ground War, and fixes (owner decisions)

## Fixes
- Move stick still drops on a quick grenade tap on iPhone (previous stale-touch-list fix did not cure it). Add a hidden touch debug overlay (Options toggle and `?touchdebug=1`) showing each live finger, its owner (left/right stick/button/spare/lost), and the last ~12 raw touch events with their `touches` and `changedTouches` ids, so the owner can screenshot the moment it fails. Also audit non-controls code for this: js/ui/tap.js listeners (new UI sound hook), HUD elements under the throw finger, anything calling `setEnabled(false)`/`#release`, focus or selection changes.
- Results screen shows after the first solo match only. Reproduce with 4 consecutive matches and fix; keep the test.
- Rematch after a Random map/mode pick stays random.
- Explosions set off mines (and chain to other mines in range).

## Pro perks (challenge based)
Each of the 15 perks has a Pro version unlocked by 2 to 3 challenge steps tied to what the perk does, about 5 to 8 matches of focused use each. Bot matches count. Pro adds a related extra ability, not a doubled main effect. Gold Pro trim wherever perks show. Veterans are meant to shine; no Pro toggle.

## Veteran identity
Calling cards earned from Pro perks, mastery camos, prestige and challenges; equipped card shows on the killer card and results. Roster intro and lobby show a Veteran / Recruit tag.

## Ground War (16v16)
Large new map (about 2.5x Railyard's area) with lanes, open ground and interiors. Modes: Ground War TDM and Ground War Domination (5 flags). Team size per mode. Budget: character LOD at distance, snapshot interest management (distant players less often), kill feed and minimap filtering, bots cheap at 30 on the host.

## Round 2
- Daily and weekly challenges: rotating XP goals (3 daily, 3 weekly) drawn from a pool that fits the game's systems; bot matches count; local clock; shown on the title and in results.
- Tutorial match: short guided bot match for first-time players covering moving, aiming and firing ring, slide, throws, streaks; offered on first run and from How to play; skippable.
- Invite links: the host shares a link (Web Share sheet, copy fallback) that opens the game straight into joining that room code.
- Match history: last 20 matches with map, mode, result, K/D, score, guns used, XP; from the existing telemetry/awards data; a detail view.
- New modes: Free-for-All (every player for themselves, 8 players on small maps, 12 on Harvest) and Gun Game (FFA, a set gun ladder advanced by kills, knife-free: final gun is the Verdict revolver, demote on being killed by equipment). Both in the picker, vote, random and bots.
