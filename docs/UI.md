# UI overhaul: AAA pass (owner: "pop and polish, eye grabbing at every scene")

## Direction: "Switchyard after dark"
Keep the established identity and push it to premium: a freight yard at dusk. Steel and ballast darks, sodium-lamp orange as the hero accent, team blue and red as signal lamps, chalk white text. Stencil display type for titles and numbers, a clean condensed face for body. Everything feels like heavy, engineered hardware: cut corners, bevelled plates, rivet and hazard-stripe details, subtle grain, light that sweeps across metal.

## Rules
- **One system.** Colors, type scale, spacing, radii and cut corners, shadows, and motion all come from `css/tokens.css` (custom properties) and `js/ui/motion.js`. No one-off values.
- **One icon set.** `js/ui/icons.js` returns crisp original SVG icons for every weapon (silhouette per gun), category, perk, streak, tactical, lethal, mode, map, medal and UI action, drawn in one consistent style. No emoji, no letters as icons, nothing traced from real games.
- **Motion with purpose.** Staggered entrances, parallax, number roll-ups, impact flashes, sweeping light on focus. Transforms and opacity only; respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Every tap gets instant pressed feedback and a UI sound (`audio.uiClick` and kin).
- **Every screen, every state.** Loading, empty, error, locked, disabled, long names, max values, 8 iPhone sizes from 667x375 to 956x440 with safe areas. No clipped text, no overlap, nothing unreadable over the game world.
- **iPhone budget.** 60 fps on menus and HUD: no layout thrash, no large blur stacks; `backdrop-filter` only on small panels and never during a match; animate only transform/opacity; images pre-sized.
- **Gallery.** `dev/ui-gallery.html` shows every screen and HUD state side by side at chosen sizes, for review.

## How to use the foundations (design system)
**Tokens** (`css/tokens.css`, imported first by `game.css`). Use the namespaced names; the old `--ballast`, `--sodium`, `--stencil`, `--cut` still work.
- Colour: `--c-night/ballast/steel/steel-hi/plate/rail/haze/chalk/white`, hero `--c-sodium(-hi/-lo/-ink/-glow)`, teams `--c-blue*` (us) and `--c-red*` (them, also destructive), `--c-go`, `--c-gold`, `--c-dusk`. Over the world: `--c-glass`, `--c-scrim`, `--text-lift`.
- Type: `--f-stencil` (titles, numerals; caps only), `--f-plate` (small labels), `--f-body` (sentences). Sizes `--fs-micro 10 / caption 11 / body 13 / lead 15 / title 18 / heading 24 / screen 32 / display 44 / mega 72`; menus multiply by `--k`. Classes `.t-mega .t-display .t-screen .t-heading .t-title .t-label .t-tag .t-body .t-lead .t-num .t-clip` (ellipsis for long names).
- Space `--sp-0..9` (2,4,6,8,12,16,20,24,32,48), `--tap-min: 44px`.
- Plates: never rounded. `--cut-xs 4 / sm 7 / md 10 / lg 14 / xl 22`. `.cut` (top-left + bottom-right, the house shape), `.cut-tl`, `.cut-br`, `.cut-4`; set `--cut` on the element. `.plate` = steel face + bevel + lit chamfer; modifiers `.is-raised .is-focus .is-sodium .is-blue .is-red .is-glass .is-locked`. `.btn` (+ `.is-primary .is-danger .is-quiet`, `:disabled`/`aria-disabled`) has the press built in.
- Light: `--bevel`, `--bevel-hi`, `--bevel-pressed`, `--bevel-sodium`, `--edge`, `--edge-hi`, `--ring-focus` (inset, survive clip-path); `--shadow-1..3`, `--glow-sodium/blue/red` for unclipped elements only. Faces `--face-steel(-hi)/-sodium/-blue/-red/-dusk`. Details `.hazard`, `.hazard-edge`, `.rivets`, `.grain` (static noise, cheap), `.lamp` (`--lamp` colour), `.rule`.
- Motion: `--dur-instant 70 / fast 140 / base 220 / slow 360 / epic 700`, `--stagger 35ms`, `--ease-out/in/inout/snap/heavy`. Collapsed under reduced motion automatically.
- Layers: `--z-world 0, hud 10, controls 20, hud-alert 25, overlay 30, menu 40, sheet 50, toast 60, transition 70, debug 90`.

**Motion** (`js/ui/motion.js`; WAAPI, transform/opacity only; every helper returns a promise and degrades under reduced motion).
`enter(el, 'up'|'down'|'left'|'right'|'rail'|'scale'|'stamp'|'fade', {delay})`, `exit(el, kind)`, `stagger(els, kind, {step, delay, max})` (one orchestrated entrance per screen), `rollNumber(el, to, {from, duration, format, onTick})`, `press(el)` / `pop(el)` (programmatic feedback), `sweep(el)` (lamp light across a plate; host needs position:relative + clip), `shake(el, {strength})`, `flash(el, {color})`, `parallax([[el, depth]], progress)`, `screenIn/screenOut(el, {dir:'forward'|'back'})`, `swapScreens(outEl, inEl, {dir})`, `wipe(async midway => swap DOM, {dir})` (hazard wipe for menu to match), `settle(el)`, `reducedMotion()`, `setReducedMotion(bool|null)`, `DUR`, `EASE`.

**Icons** (`js/ui/icons.js`). `icon(id, {size, color, accent, square, title, cls})` returns SVG markup; `size` is the height (weapons are 2.5x as wide; `square:true` fits a weapon to a square). Colour follows `currentColor`; the accent part is sodium unless `--icon-accent` (or `accent`) says otherwise; set `--icon-accent: currentColor` for one-colour use (e.g. icons on a sodium plate). Ids: weapon ids (`ar` ... `thumper`), `cat:<Category>` (or the category name), streak/tactical/lethal/perk ids, slots `optic barrel mag grip`, `mode:<id>` (or `tdm`...), `map:<id>`, `medal:<id>` (also `headshot`), and `ui:*` (back next up down close check plus minus settings options play pause warning info help lock unlock star edit refresh share copy paste save download mic mic-off volume mute user party bot signal clock timer cup crown stats flag eye eye-off trash menu home exit join host quick random graphics controls phone aim rank prestige camo map gunsmith loadout operator crate headset skull new dot ready); bare names like `back`, `bots`, `warn` resolve too. Unknown ids get a neutral lamp, never letters. `uiIcon(name)` returns null for unknown names. `ICON_GROUPS`/`ICON_IDS` list everything. `widgets.itemMark(id)` now draws icons for weapons, perks, equipment, attachments (by slot) and camos; `widgets.countUp` delegates to `rollNumber`.

**Sound** (`js/audio/audio.js`). `audio.uiSound(kind)` with kind `tap back confirm deny tab tick unlock levelUp rankUp`, or the shorthands `uiClick/uiTap/uiBack/uiConfirm/uiDeny/uiTab/uiTick(t 0..1)/uiUnlock/uiLevelUp/uiRankUp`; `setUiVolume(0..1.5)`. Own bus below the gameplay mix, own 3-voice cap, rate-limited; safe before unlock. Wire every tap once in `main.js`: `setTapSound(kind => audio.uiSound(kind))` from `js/ui/tap.js`. Per element: `data-sound="back|confirm|deny|tab|tick|none"`; default is `back` for data-act/data-tap containing back/close/cancel/leave, `deny` for disabled controls, else `tap`. Outcomes (unlock, level up) are played by the screen, timed to its animation.

**Gallery**: `dev/ui-gallery.html` (see the page header for query options).

## In-match HUD layout (`css/hud.css`, `js/ui/hud.js` `layout()`)
The thumbs own both lower corners and the button cluster; every mode places its panels in four groups around them:
- **Top**: the score bar and the objective strip (`hud.strip.mount(node, { row, order })`, rows 0-2: state, detail such as extraction's contract or the boss bar, live progress). Modes mount into the strip instead of placing their own plates.
- **Side**: minimap, the squad or crew list beside it (fixed-height rows: name, health bar, thin plates bar; down is red with a down mark, out is greyed), and the kill feed under both (two or three rows, as many as fit above the move stick). On the aim side, the corner chips and the weapon panel under them, never over the aim stick.
- **Dock**: health beside the streak tiles (or the battle royale's plate and medkit) on the bottom edge between the sticks.
- **Lane**: one prompt at a time over the dock (take a gun, exfil, the bomb, a flag, Graveyard Shift's use button).

Out and spectating, the weapon panel and health hide and the out plate takes the strip's second row. The FPS overlay is one line in the move stick's bottom corner. Feed rows size to content: names ellipsize first, the words after them stay. The pass runs on a new match, rotation, HUD size and hand changes, never per frame. `dev/ui-hud-layout-test.py` checks every mode at five iPhone sizes, both hands and three HUD sizes for stick, button and panel overlap.

A custom touch layout (Options > Accessibility, docs/ACCESSIBILITY.md) is placed by `js/ui/layout.js placeControls` before the pass measures the controls, so the pass works from wherever the player put them; placed streak tiles leave the dock and count as a button. The editor keeps controls out of the top band the HUD's top and side groups use.
