# Owner's stick report, 2026-11-19 (build 1119-5)
Replay: matches the phone at every step (dev/touch-replay.mjs). What happened, at ~104.9 s:
1. Left thumb #..214 steering at (307, 344); right thumb #..215 on the Tactical button (smoke).
2. Right thumb lifts (touchend #..215) -> smoke thrown (Q tactical smoke, left 0).
3. 10 ms later iOS sends **touchcancel for the LEFT thumb** (#..214, target div.controls) with an empty touch list.
   controls.js keeps the stick ("kept through a touchcancel 10 ms after a release").
4. iOS then sends **no events at all** for #..214 for 1.2 s although the thumb is still down: the stick holds its last
   direction (frozen), so to the player it stops responding.
5. A new touch at (449, 187) takes the stick ("unseen 1224 ms: new thumb takes it"); then the 3-finger report gesture.
So the cause is iOS cancelling the move thumb right after the tactical button release, after which iOS never delivers
that finger again. Next: find what the release does that makes iOS cancel other touches (DOM change on the button as the
count hits 0? a click/focus? a gesture recognizer), and prevent it; meanwhile a cancelled stick should let go (stop
moving) at once instead of freezing on its last direction, and re-anchor on the next touch anywhere on the left half.
